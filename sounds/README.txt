Instrument recordings from the Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra by Mattias Westlund
(https://github.com/peastman/sso): chorus, 1st violins (sustained, plucked and tremolo),
celli, double basses (plucked), trombones, trumpets, flute, oboe, marimba, grand piano,
harp, celeste, timpani, shaker, conga and cymbals.

Licence: Creative Commons Sampling Plus 1.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/sampling+/1.0/

Changes made: a selection of the original notes was converted to mono AAC; all except the
chorus were trimmed, faded out and levelled. File names are MIDI note numbers (60 = middle C).
